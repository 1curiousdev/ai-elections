# Fictional candidate packet

Each file contains the article, speech, and debate Q&A shown to the models for one candidate. These are original fictional records. Names, parties, demographic details, and the location were omitted from the prompt. The short descriptions below are for readers and were outside the model prompt.

| Candidate | Data center expansion | Safety and speed | Limits on big labs | Open versus closed weights | Worker protection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [A](A.md) | **Conditional support.** Pause new very large sites for nine months while setting grid and water standards. | **Targeted release holds.** Ask higher governments to stop releases that fail independent frontier tests. | **Supports limits.** Shared testing, short contracts, and data portability. | **Neutral on format.** Equal credit if safeguards and exit plans are equivalent. | **Supports notice and paid training** under city contracts. |
| [B](B.md) | **Strong support.** Accelerate permits and tie rebates to investment and apprenticeships. | **Opposes a broad slowdown.** Uses reporting and liability after deployment. | **Weak support for limits.** Relies on large labs’ scale and would rebid contracts. | **Prefers closed by default.** Uses managed services for most departments. Allows open bids with a staffing plan and a measurable benefit. | **Supports training and placement.** Scales programs with tax receipts. |
| [C](C.md) | **Opposes new hyperscale sites on the shared grid.** Backs smaller sites and shared public compute. | **Supports deployment delays for major workforce impacts.** Would require a funded worker transition. | **Strong support for limits.** Split contracts, preserve portability, and maintain fallback vendors. | **Moderate preference for open.** Extra credit for qualified open bids in routine work when the city can maintain a fallback. | **Strong support.** Bargaining, paid training, and a two-year wage bridge for workers displaced by city contracts. |
| [D](D.md) | **Conditional support.** Approve a few large projects with power and water secured up front. | **Supports continued research and targeted deployment checks.** Requires independent review for critical services. | **Weak support for limits.** No exclusivity and portable data. Strict requirements may favor large vendors. | **Prefers closed in critical services.** Open bids can compete for low-risk work or demonstrate equivalent accountability and control. | **Limited support.** Track losses and issue vouchers after verified displacement. |
| [E](E.md) | **Supports smaller sites and shared compute.** Reviews large sites case by case. | **Opposes a broad slowdown.** Requires evidence before use in benefits, hiring, or utilities. | **Strong support for limits.** Divide contracts, make switching easier, and fund shared testing. | **Prefers open for suitable work.** Starts with locally runnable options and buys closed where performance or cost warrants it. | **Supports a small fellowship.** Expansion requires employer co-funding. |
